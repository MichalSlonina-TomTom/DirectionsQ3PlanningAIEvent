# Directions AI Engineering Championship Program

The PU Directions Engineering Leadership Team has decided to roll out an intensive **Directions AI Engineering Championship** program in Q2. This is a PU Directions specific extension of the TomTom-wide AI Engineering Program, which you all have heard about now.

## Goal

To significantly improve the chance of every engineer in PU Directions to jump on board of the ongoing inevitable, revolutionary and very fast change of how Software Engineering is done: **AI Engineering**.

## Why

We, the Engineering Leadership Team, want everyone to successfully learn and adopt AI Engineering and see giving this opportunity as the core of our responsibility. The EMs among us are responsible for the professional development of the Software Engineers who report to them; the Staff Engineers among us deeply care about multiplying efficient and productive tools and ways of working.

## Principal approach

- Take away the fear / entry barrier of AI Engineering
- Create preconditions for all the engineers to remain (reasonably) up to speed with the fast development of the new tools.

## How specifically

- Each Engineering Team must select the Team's **AI Engineering Champion**. Preferably this should be an engineer who is already excited about AI Engineering, but does not have to be.
- Each Staff Engineer (optionally, including "acting" Staff Engineers) will also become **(Staff) AI Engineering Champion**, acting on Product Team / Functional Area level.
- Every AI Engineering Champion (including Staff) gets a specific **"AI Engineering day"** in the week (unless decided otherwise by the EM, it will be the meeting-free Wednesday), on which the person will be explicitly and fully freed up from any duty apart from AI Engineering Championship. Those duties are (the last 2 are explicitly dedicated to reduce the fear/entry barrier):
    - Learn, learn, learn, and practice the AI Engineering - also from other AI Engineering Champions (typically Staffs) - including identifying tools/"skills" that are useful for the team
    - Coach other colleagues (propagate ways of working by cascading knowledge - multiplying):
        - Team members, for Team AI Engineering Champion
        - Team AI Engineering Champions, for Staff AI Engineering Champions
    - (not limited to the AI Engineering day) Drive adoption in the team: show by example and by joint practical sessions with the people how AI Engineering is useful for the specific tasks / epics / features developed by the team - very much hands on!
- Every EM will prioritize adoption and facilitation of this process in their team / PT, including getting the appropriate licenses. The specific ways of working (e.g. how to distribute time between the different AI Engineering Champion duties, prioritize tasks suitable for AI Engineering etc.) are in the responsibility of the individual EMs.
- Every Software Engineer works on achieving the Q2 goals, while applying AI Engineering (including implementation of tools that are supposed to make work more efficient). Important: please actively engage with your AI Engineering Champion(s) and with your manager sharing the successes and failures and getting guidance.
- We will use a dedicated Slack channel with all the EMs and the AI Engineering Champions (**#directions-ai-engineering-program**) to roll it out and monitor progress / concerns / blockers (including knowledge exchange on getting the right licenses, bringing learnings, etc.).

> **Note:** To anyone that may feel that this will cause slow-down of development in Q2 and failure on some deadlines - risk accepted. I don't think it will happen though - AI Engineering practices can make Software Engineering faster already soon after seriously trying it out, with everything I learned about it so far. So, the Q2 plan remains unaffected by this Program. To be clear, I also don't expect faster delivery in Q2 as a result of this program; the Q2 focus is to learn - but I will celebrate positive surprises in this regard!
